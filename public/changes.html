v1.1 13 November 2026

  • Loudness by aircraft type and flight phase. Jets are louder behind than ahead; helicopters louder descending.
  • Distant aircraft are predicted louder: standard ground and air losses, by engine type.
  • Weather: low aircraft can be louder on still nights, and up to 3 dB quieter upwind.
  • New setting: Warehouse, for a lightweight or converted stage.
  • What the microphone learned starts over.
  • Patch notes, and a link to the source.
