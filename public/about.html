Hold for Sound

About

  • A personal experiment by one person. Not a commercial service. Free.
  • It may change or stop at any time.
  • Not for safety, or for any decision that matters.
  • Times and loudness are estimates only. The loudness numbers are guesses, not measurements.
  • Flight data can be late, wrong or missing. Aircraft without a transponder are not shown.
  • Provided as is, with no warranty of any kind.
  • Some of the data sources allow non-commercial use only.

Privacy

Without sharing

  • Your settings, the address you typed and what the microphone learned stay in this browser's local storage.
  • If this site asks for a password, one cookie remembers that you entered it, for up to a year. No other cookies.
  • Microphone sound is analysed in this browser. It is never recorded, stored or sent. Listening starts only when you press its button.
  • Your device's location is asked for only when you press Use my location.
  • To find aircraft, the server sends the location, rounded to about 100 m, to adsb.lol (or adsb.fi), and to Open-Meteo for its elevation. While live aircraft are watched, the server also sends those rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo for weather, at most once an hour per cached place, including failed attempts.
  • An address you type is sent to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) to find it. Typed coordinates are not.
  • Map images come from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address and the area shown.
  • Traffic to this site passes through Cloudflare.
  • The server counts visits. It holds IP addresses in memory for up to a day, to count visits and limit abuse, and never writes them to disk.
  • Addresses looked up, and what was found, are held in the server's memory for up to a day to answer repeats. To limit abuse, the places watched and addresses looked up from each IP address are held for 10 minutes. None of this is written to disk.

Contact form

  • When you send it, your name, email, category and message go to the developer by email. Nothing of it is kept on this server, only the time it was sent, for a day, to limit how many are sent.
  • When the check is on, opening the contact form loads a Cloudflare Turnstile check, which sees your IP address and browser details; your IP address is also sent with your message, to verify the check.

With Share data with the developer on

  • Sent: the set's coordinates, the page's predictions, your marks, sound levels, and a detailed spectrogram of the sound: from 30 s before to 30 s after each predicted aircraft, and to a minute after each mark; and from 10 s before to 10 s after other aircraft-like noise lasting 20 s or more, up to 15 min an hour. The spectrogram is not a recording, but sounds near the microphone, speech included, can show in it. Audio is never sent.
  • Your approximate connection region (from Cloudflare) and rounded searched locations with lookup results are held for about 24 hours, without the addresses typed.
  • Session files are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Only the developer sees them. They are never sold or shared.
  • Switch sharing off at any time.
  • To have a session deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository with its session ID, shown beside the switch while sharing.
